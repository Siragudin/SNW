# Simulation‑Native Worlds  
### GameAI, Intent and the Architecture of Living Digital Reality

> “Don’t play worlds. Live in them.”

---

## 📖 Overview
**Simulation‑Native Worlds** is an architectural framework for building digital environments that behave like real ones.  
Not through scripts.  
Not through handcrafted triggers.  
But through **matter**, **agents**, and **intentions** — three layers that form a unified, causal, living world.

This repository contains the conceptual foundation behind the trilogy of books exploring how digital worlds can evolve beyond static sets and become environments that exist on their own.

---

## 🌐 Core Principles

### **1. Matter as the Foundation**
Everything in the world is made of matter with properties:
- temperature  
- density  
- moisture  
- strength  
- friction  
- deformation  
- destruction  

Matter is not decoration.  
Matter *behaves*.

### **2. Agents as Material Bodies**
NPCs and players are not logical entities with tags — they are **bodies**:
- with mass  
- with fatigue  
- with grip  
- with limits  
- with consequences  

NPCs perceive geometry, processes, danger, and opportunity.  
They act inside the same physics as the player.

### **3. Intentions as the Interface**
The player does not press “interact.”  
They express **intent**:
- “break the wall”  
- “remove the wheel”  
- “start a fire”  

Intent AI interprets meaning, selects primitives, and executes actions through physics.

### **4. Adaptive Simulation**
The world never pauses.  
Near the player — full detail.  
Far away — abstract but honest processes:
- fire spreads  
- water erodes  
- metal rusts  
- wood rots  
- NPCs work, build, repair, destroy  

The world keeps living even when no one is watching.

---

## 🔥 What This Architecture Enables

- **Real fire** that spreads with wind, dies on wet wood, creates smoke, and triggers rain.  
- **Real water** that flows, pools, erodes, floods, and reshapes terrain.  
- **Real destruction** based on load, stress, weak points, and material fatigue.  
- **Real NPCs** that remember, plan, learn, act, and change the world.  
- **Real consequences** that persist for days, weeks, seasons.  
- **Real creativity** through engineering, ecology, and matter — not recipes or grids.

This is not “better physics.”  
This is a **new ontology** for digital worlds.
